    FAQ

    Corporate Majdoor, Explained

    What is Corporate Majdoor?

    Corporate Majdoor is an ambient radio experience created for Indian techies and 9-to-5 employees, featuring nostalgic Bollywood work tunes, relatable meeting doodles and funny corporate dialogues.

    Can I listen while working or coding?

    Yes! The radio is curated with relaxing, low-distraction retro Bollywood melodies that keep you focused through meetings, debugging and spreadsheet crunches. Add rain or keyboard ambience for extra flow.

    Is the live chat public?

    It is. Anyone on the site sees the room. Pick any display name, keep it friendly — abusive words are auto-masked and spam is rate-limited.

    What happens in "Boss Aaya!" mode?

    The whole screen instantly becomes a very serious looking IDE: file explorer, sprint tickets, a payment-gateway class typing itself out and a build terminal. Press ESC to return to the radio.

    Do you own the music?

    No. Tracks stream from the rights-holders' official YouTube uploads through the YouTube IFrame Player API, and every track shows its credit on screen.

    How do I switch themes?

    Use the theme dropdown at the top of the screen — Corporate Majdoor, Deluxe Saloon, Midnight Overtime or Tapri Sunset. Your choice is remembered on this device.
